PARUL UNIVERSITY


Parul Universityis a leading private multidisciplinary university located in Vadodara, Gujarat. Established in 2015, it evolved from the Parul Group of Institutes (founded in 1993). Renowned for its massive 150+ acre campus, robust industry placements, and global student community, it offers over 200 programs across various disciplines.

Key heighlights :-

  1. Accreditations & Rankings: Holds a prestigious NAAC A++ accreditation and is ranked in the top 150 overall by NIRF. It is also recognized as a Centre of Excellence by the Government of Gujarat
  2. Student Diversity: Houses over 70,000 students, including a strong international presence of 6,000+ students from 75+ countries.
  3. Placements: Hosts over 2,200 recruiting companies with a reported highest package of ₹60 LPA. Top recruiters include major brands like Google, Amazon, Infosys, and TCS.
  4. Campus & Facilities: Features a modern, self-contained campus with specialized academic blocks, advanced laboratories, extensive sports complexes, in-campus residential facilities, and a dedicated e-rickshaw transportation system.
  5. Global Exposure: Promotes internationalization with over 120 global academic and industry tie-ups, facilitating student study tours and international faculty exchange programs
